# Sbuddy

Sbuddy helps students study past competition math problems: browse them, add them to study plans, and review them with spaced repetition.

## Language

### Competition content

**Competition**:
A family of contests that share a format, such as MATHCOUNTS or AMC 10.
_Avoid_: Test category, test type, exam type

**Test**:
One published sitting of a Competition, such as "MATHCOUNTS 2025 Chapter" or "AMC 10A 2025". Each MATHCOUNTS level (School, Chapter, State, National, Practice) and each numbered practice set is a separate Test. The level is recorded as the Test's variant, as AMC's A and B are; it is not a layer of its own.
_Avoid_: Exam, paper, contest

**Round**:
A separately administered part of a Test, such as Sprint, Target, Team or Countdown. A Test with no named parts, such as an AMC 10, has a single Round.
_Avoid_: Component, section, part

**Round format**:
The rules a Round is given under: problem count, time limit, calculator use, and individual or team. Recorded on each Test for reference; nothing depends on it yet.
_Avoid_: Round rules, round config

**Problem**:
One numbered question in a Round, identified by its Test, Round and number.
_Avoid_: Question, item

**Answer**:
The official result of a Problem, revealed after an attempt and graded by the student, never checked automatically. A Problem may also have accepted alternative forms of its Answer.
_Avoid_: Solution, key, correct answer

**Answer unit**:
The unit printed with a Problem's answer blank, such as "cm²" or "years old". It is part of how the Answer is read, not part of the Answer itself; most go after the number, but currency such as "$" goes before it.
_Avoid_: Suffix, label

**Solution**:
A worked explanation of how to reach the Answer, which may include figures. A Problem has zero or more Solutions.
_Avoid_: Explanation, answer
